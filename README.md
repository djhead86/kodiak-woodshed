# Bass Woodshed

A practice tool for bass players. One page, no install, no account. Open it, hit Space, play.

**Live:** https://djhead86.github.io/kodiak-woodshed/

## Metronome
- Any meter from 1 to 15 beats over /4 or /8
- Accent groupings (`2+2+3`, `3+3+2`, ...) with one-click presets for the meter you're in
- Per-beat accent, normal, or mute (click a beat to cycle)
- Subdivisions: 2, 3, or 4 clicks per beat
- Speed trainer: start BPM, target BPM, +N every M bars
- Tap tempo

## Neck map
- 4, 5, and 6 string, with standard and drop tunings (Drop D, E♭, D standard, Drop C, Drop A, High C, B♭)
- 15 scales and modes, including harmonic/melodic minor, Phrygian dominant, Hungarian minor, and double harmonic
- **Scale + chord:** click any diatonic triad or 7th to overlay its chord tones on the scale
- **Arpeggio:** 17 chord types on any root
- Labels: notes, notes + intervals, or intervals (m3/M3 or ♭3/3 style), counted from the chord or the key
- **Box mode:** restrict the board to a 4 to 6 fret window and step through positions
- Click any note to hear it

## Keys
| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Space | Start / stop |
| T | Tap tempo |
| ↑ / ↓ | BPM ±1 (Shift ±5) |
| [ / ] | Move the box down / up the neck |

## Run locally
It's a single `index.html` with no build step. Open it in a browser. Settings are saved in your browser's local storage.

Part of [Kodiak Den](https://kodiak-den.com).
